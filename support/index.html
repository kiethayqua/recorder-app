Purchases and subscriptions

How do I restore Pro on a new or reset iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro, then open Recorder and either:

  • go to the Settings tab and tap Restore Purchases, or
  • open any Pro feature and tap Restore at the bottom of the plans screen.
How do I cancel my subscription?

Apple manages subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Select Recorder and tap Cancel Subscription.

While Pro is active, you can also open this screen from Recorder’s Settings tab with Manage Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

How does the free trial work?

New subscribers to the yearly plan get one week free. If you don’t cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the yearly subscription starts automatically at the price shown on the plans screen. The plans screen shows whether you are eligible before you subscribe.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Sign in, choose the purchase and select “Request a refund”.

Is the lifetime purchase a subscription?

No. Lifetime is a one-time payment that unlocks Pro permanently on every device signed in to your Apple Account. It never renews.

Recording

Recorder can’t use the microphone

Open the iPhone Settings app and go to Privacy & Security › Microphone, then turn on Recorder. If another app is using the microphone, such as a phone or video call, finish that first.

What do Raw and Enhanced mean?

Raw is the microphone signal with no processing. Enhanced removes steady background noise, like fans, traffic or air conditioning, and evens out speech levels. Pro users can save both at once and compare them during playback.

Which noise reduction setting should I use?

Balanced suits most rooms. Choose Natural for quiet spaces where you want the voice untouched, and Strong for loud, steady noise. Noise reduction is tuned for speech, so use Raw when recording music.

Can I use an external microphone?

Yes. Connect a wired, USB or Bluetooth microphone, then tap the input menu at the top of the Record screen and choose it. If it isn’t listed, tap Find available inputs. If you change the input during a recording, the recording stops safely and is saved.

What happens when a free recording reaches 30 minutes?

The recording stops and is saved normally. Nothing is lost. You can start a new recording right away, or upgrade to Pro for unlimited length.

My recording stopped when a call came in

iOS gives phone calls and some system alerts priority over the microphone. When that happens, Recorder stops and saves everything captured up to that moment. Start a new recording after the interruption.

A recording is marked “Recoverable”

The previous session ended unexpectedly, for example because the app was closed or the device restarted mid-recording. Recorder keeps the original audio, so you can still open, play and export it from the Library.

Files and storage

Where are my recordings stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. Recordings are never uploaded, and they are not included in iCloud or device backups. Deleting the app deletes your recordings, so export anything you want to keep first.

How do I export or back up a recording?

Open the recording in the Library and choose Share M4A, or Share WAV with Pro. Then save it to Files, AirDrop it to a Mac or send it to another app. You can also swipe right on a recording in the Library to share it quickly as M4A.

How much can I record?

It depends on free space. The Record screen shows an estimate of the recording time left on your iPhone. Saving both Raw and Enhanced uses about twice the space of one track. Recorder stops safely if storage runs very low.
