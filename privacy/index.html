Privacy Policy

Recorder for iPhone · Effective October 9, 2026

In short: Recorder does not collect, store on a server or share any of your data. Your recordings are created, processed and kept on your iPhone, and they leave it only when you choose to share them.

Who we are

Recorder (“the app”) is developed by Kiet Nguyen (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles information. If you have questions, contact us at nguyenkiet193@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs. It does not send your recordings, notes, titles or usage information to us or to anyone else.

Microphone and recordings

  • The app uses your microphone only while you are recording. iOS asks for your permission first, and you can change it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.
  • Noise reduction runs entirely on your device. No audio is uploaded for processing.
  • Recordings, along with the titles, notes and technical details (such as the input device name, duration and audio format), are stored in the app’s private storage on your iPhone and protected by iOS file encryption.
  • Recordings are excluded from iCloud and device backups. If you delete the app, its recordings are deleted with it, so export anything you want to keep first.

Sharing and exports

When you tap Share, the app creates a temporary copy of the recording and opens the iOS share sheet. The destination you choose, such as Messages, Mail or Files, then handles the file under its own privacy policy. The app deletes temporary copies after sharing, or the next time it starts.

Purchases

Recorder Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app receives only confirmation of which purchases are active, so that it can unlock Pro features. Apple’s handling of this information is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Settings and diagnostics

Your preferences, such as recording mode, input and appearance, are stored only on your device. The “Local diagnostics” option keeps any diagnostic information on your device. It is never sent to us.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect or hold your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. You control your data directly. You can delete individual recordings in the app’s Library, delete everything by removing the app, and revoke microphone access in iOS Settings. If you have questions about privacy laws that apply to you, such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact us at the address above.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
